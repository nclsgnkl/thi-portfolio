# Smart Home & Guest Experience Platform

> ℹ️ **Note:** This README was created with the assistance of AI and reviewed by me. The project, its concept and the work presented here are my own.

## About the Project

The Guest Experience Platform is an independent project exploring how technology can improve the experience of guests in hotels and short-term accommodation.

Most existing accommodation management systems are primarily designed around the needs of property managers: organizing bookings, managing properties and simplifying operational processes. I wanted to approach the problem from the other side: **What if the guest experience was at the center of the platform?**

The idea is to provide guests with a single, intuitive interface that brings together everything they might need during their stay. This includes accommodation information such as check-in instructions, Wi-Fi details and waste disposal, as well as local recommendations, smart home controls and additional services like extending their stay.

## Concept & Implementation

I started by designing the main screen in Figma, establishing the initial visual direction and user interface. As the project evolved, I expanded the design with the help of Claude Design, refining and adapting individual pages to match my ideas.

The application is developed using an AI-native, specification-driven workflow. I created custom AI agents inspired by the BMAD methodology to support different stages of planning, implementation and refinement. Rather than relying on unstructured prompting, I use specifications to guide development and keep the different parts of the application consistent.

From the beginning, I also considered technical foundations such as internationalization and light and dark mode, so that these aspects would be integrated into the architecture rather than added later.

The result is a functional application that brings the concept to life, currently using mock data.
