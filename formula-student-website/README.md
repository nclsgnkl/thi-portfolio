# Formula Student Website

> ℹ️ **Note:** This README was created with the assistance of AI and reviewed by me. The project and the work presented here are my own contributions to a collaborative project.

## About the Project

As part of the Formula Student team at OTH Regensburg, we decided to completely redesign our website. The previous website had become outdated, so we started from scratch with the goal of creating a more modern and structured online presence for the team.

## My Contribution

Together with a fellow team member, I worked on the website's overall visual direction and design system. We developed the initial design guidelines collaboratively and then worked mostly independently on individual pages, exchanging ideas and feedback along the way.

My contributions included designing several subpages, such as:

- **News:** A page presenting team updates and newsletter content (until 2024)
- **Garage:** An overview of the cars built by the team
- **Recruiting:** A page introducing the team and providing information for prospective members

The website was implemented using WordPress and its existing building blocks, allowing us to translate our designs into a working website without custom development.

## Result

The website is still actively used by the Formula Student team and continues to be developed further.
